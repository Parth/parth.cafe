At the current moment, somehow AI has managed to be something that is both the subject of outrage, and immense excitement.

This is going to be an attempt to index the various perspectives I've heard and share my perspective. 

# Outrage

## Energy Usage
AI energy usage. This reminds me of the stuff that was going on with Proof of Work. The people consuming the energy paid for that energy. I think it would be a more sound argument for the outrage to be directed towards how that energy is produced or towards any cronyism taking place.

## Circular Finance
The financial bubble. To me this seems likely, but also not a moral issue. We have the freedom to short these companies. I too would be outraged if the government bailed out AI companies, but my outrage would be mostly directed to the government. If you're upset about large institutions exposing themselves to AI, you can just move your money elsewhere.

## IP
Intellectual Property. Now things are getting more compelling for me. It seems a shame that much of the training data was produced by people that won't be compensated for their efforts. [StackOverflow](https://meta.stackoverflow.com/questions/437921/how-does-the-continued-decline-in-posts-since-may-25-influence-our-interpretati) is the most clear example of this. SO was a huge contributor to training data for these LLMs, and LLMs will be a huge contributor to the death of StackOverflow. I think likely there are examples across many industries where this is the case. It seems like LLMs will be an upgrade to the process of the sort of knowledge that SO was an aggregator for. You don't learn the fundementals of computer science on SO, you learn how to get past the peculiarity of your situation. LLMs present a large upgrade to that experience, a huge amount of your context is preserved, the LLM can search deep across the internet for a solution, try it for you and genuinely save you huge swaths of time. But SO provided us more than that, it provided a place and incentive for experienced people to contribute knowledge and help other people. But the currency, unfortunately was never real.
