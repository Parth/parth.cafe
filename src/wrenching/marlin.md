# Measurements needed

Dropper post sizing: target **31.6 mm diameter**, internally actuated post; travel remains undecided until measured. Fit measurements do not establish the structural safety of modifying the frame.

* [ ] Riding height: **_____ mm** — with the saddle at normal pedaling height, measure along the seatpost from the top of the frame's seatpost clamp to the center of the saddle rails, not the saddle surface.
* [ ] Available insertion depth: **_____ mm** — mark the current saddle/post position, remove the post, and measure from the same clamp reference to the first obstruction or bend. Inspect for bottle bosses; a thin rod can pass an obstruction that the full-width post cannot.
* [ ] Usable insertion depth for OneUp sizing: **_____ mm** — subtract 10–15 mm from the measured depth as OneUp's initial cable-clearance allowance; confirm sufficient space for the selected post's actuator and a smooth housing bend.
* [ ] Final fit check: selected post reaches normal pedaling height fully extended without being too tall, fits within available insertion depth, and meets both frame and post minimum insertion requirements. Record model/travel: **_____**.

Reference: [OneUp measurement guide and sizing calculator](https://www.oneupcomponents.com/pages/dropper-post-length-selector). Use each manufacturer's own dimensions and clearance requirements when comparing other posts. For the planned 1x setup, allow for a compatible left under-bar lever with a separate 22.2 mm handlebar clamp, cable and housing.
